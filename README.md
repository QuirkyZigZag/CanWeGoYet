# CanWeGoYet
GlobalJam 2026 submisison


You play as an antisocial friend acting as designated driver.

Your goal is to not get too drained but also not ruin your reputation until you can leave at midnight

Match peoples social masks to interact with them without draining as much social battery and gaining likeability points

People with smiley faces are social rechargers and do not need any specific mask

can be found to play here: https://globalgamejam.org/games/2026?
